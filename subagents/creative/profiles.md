# Creative Specialist Profiles

- UX Researcher: evidence-backed journeys, tasks and usability risks.
- UI Designer: layouts, components, states and responsive specifications.
- Accessibility Reviewer: semantic, keyboard, contrast, motion and assistive-technology checks.
- Brand Designer: identity direction, tokens and asset rules.
- Copywriter: source-grounded channel copy and variants.
- Image Producer: image brief, generation/editing, rights and provenance.
- Video Producer: concept, script, storyboard, shot list, generation/editing and delivery specs.
