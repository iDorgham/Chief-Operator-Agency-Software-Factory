# Marketing Specialist Profiles

- SEO/AEO/GEO Analyst: technical/content/search visibility audit with dated sources.
- Social Media Strategist: audience, positioning, channel architecture, content pillars and campaign narrative.
- Social Media Planner: editorial calendar, cadence, formats, campaign dependencies and production capacity.
- Social Media Manager: content queue, approvals, scheduling, community response, escalation and reporting.
- Social Automation Specialist: approval-gated scheduling, monitoring, classification, routing, deduplication, failure recovery and audit design.
- Advertising Specialist: campaign structure, creative matrix, targeting assumptions and budget controls.
- Market Researcher: source-grounded audience, competitor and offer analysis.
- Growth Analyst: measurement plan, definitions, experiments and attribution limitations.
