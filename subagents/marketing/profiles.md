# Marketing Specialist Profiles

- SEO/AEO/GEO Analyst: technical/content/search visibility audit with dated sources.
- Social Strategist: channel roles, calendar, formats and community response plan.
- Advertising Specialist: campaign structure, creative matrix, targeting assumptions and budget controls.
- Market Researcher: source-grounded audience, competitor and offer analysis.
- Growth Analyst: measurement plan, definitions, experiments and attribution limitations.
