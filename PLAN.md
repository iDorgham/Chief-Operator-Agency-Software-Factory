# Master Implementation Plan

Status: Phase 1 implemented; later phases require separate approval.

## Goal

Deliver a professional AI organization with one Chief Operator, seven persistent leads, bounded specialist subagents, structured memory, reusable skills, explicit approvals and a future Control Desk backed by an authoritative Factory Core.

## Phases

1. **Foundation — current:** archive legacy material; establish governance, hierarchy, rules, contracts, plans, skills and synthetic fixtures.
2. **OpenBot configuration:** verify version and account capabilities; import the Chief Operator and leads; test private handoffs with synthetic data; activate and verify a commercial license before any company or client work.
3. **Factory Core:** implement projects, tasks, runs, events, approvals, artifacts, locks and adapter state using the architecture decision process.
4. **Control Desk:** implement the operator dashboard, Demo/Live separation, approvals inbox, audit feed, progress and recovery.
5. **Software integration:** connect one test GitHub repository; prove idempotent branch/PR/CI/review flow without production merge.
6. **Creative and growth integration:** connect approved design, media, analytics and publishing services one at a time; retain exact approval gates.
7. **Operations and improvement:** measure quality, time, waits, retries and cost where visible; apply only approved reversible proposals.

## Phase gates

- A phase starts only when its plan, dependencies, risk and acceptance criteria are approved.
- Live integrations remain disabled until synthetic workflows pass.
- Provider/model availability, privacy, commercial rights and costs are verified from live selectors.
- Synthetic MCP and agent verification may proceed without a commercial license, but company work, client work and other commercial use remain blocked until OpenBot Billing shows the appropriate license as current.
- Architecture decisions with lock-in, cost or data consequences require an ADR and operator approval.
