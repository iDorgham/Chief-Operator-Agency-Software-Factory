# Factory Product Plan

## Primary user

Solo operator running a professional software/creative/marketing agency through one Chief Operator.

## Jobs

- Turn unclear objectives into approved briefs and plans.
- Route work without choosing among many bots manually.
- See accountable owner, progress, blockers, evidence and approvals.
- Produce software and marketing/creative deliverables safely.
- Learn from completed work without autonomous policy drift.

## V1 non-goals

Autonomous production deployment, unrestricted publishing, automatic ad spend, self-editing governance, multi-tenant billing and broad CRM replacement.
