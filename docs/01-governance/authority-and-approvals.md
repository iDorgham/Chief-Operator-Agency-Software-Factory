# Authority and Approval Matrix

| Action | Proposer | Reviewer | Final authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Approve product scope | Product Lead | Delivery/technical leads | Operator |
| Implement bounded task | Software or Creative Lead | Relevant Quality/Marketing lead | Approved spec |
| Accept evidence | Quality Lead | Product owner | Chief Operator for internal completion |
| Send client message | Client-facing lead | Chief Operator | Operator |
| Publish/schedule content | Marketing Lead | Claims/brand review | Operator |
| Start/change ad spend | Marketing Lead | Operations/Chief Operator | Operator |
| Deploy production | Software Lead | Quality/Release Lead | Operator |
| Merge or delete branch | Software/Quality Lead | Repository policy | Operator when required |
| Change credentials/access | Relevant lead | Security/Chief Operator | Operator |
| Amend factory policy | Operations Lead | Affected leads | Operator |

Approval records must identify the exact action, target, artifact version, impact, approver and timestamp. Broad project approval does not approve every consequential action.
