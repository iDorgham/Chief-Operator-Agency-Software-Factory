# Quality Gates

## Common

Approved scope, owner, sources, acceptance criteria, permissions, limitations and independent review are visible.

## Software

Repository/base/head identity is verified; tests are reported accurately; security, privacy, performance and accessibility are reviewed proportionally; current-head required checks are green; merge/deploy approval remains separate.

## Creative and marketing

Audience, objective, channel and CTA are defined; claims map to sources; brand/accessibility checks pass; asset rights and generation provenance are recorded; exact publish/spend action is approved.

## Analytics

Source, date range, timezone and definitions are stated; measured data, estimates and interpretation are separated; missing data is labeled.
