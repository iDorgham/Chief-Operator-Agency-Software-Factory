# Capability Map

| Domain | Accountable lead | Delivery mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strategy, product, architecture | Product Lead | Persistent lead + ADR/spec skills |
| Planning and management | Delivery Manager | Persistent lead + planning/handoff skills |
| Frontend/backend/database | Software Lead | Temporary specialists + builder skills |
| Web/dashboard/desktop/mobile | Software Lead | Stack-specific specialist profiles |
| Security/DevOps/performance/QA | Quality Lead | Independent subagents and review skills |
| Brand/UI/UX/design systems | Design and Content Lead | Persistent lead + design skills |
| Text/image/video | Design and Content Lead | Production skills and approved media tools |
| Social-media strategy | Marketing Lead | Audience, positioning, channel roles and content pillars |
| Social-media planning | Marketing Lead | Editorial calendar, campaigns, formats, cadence and resource plan |
| Social-media management | Marketing Lead | Production queue, community policy, approvals, reporting and issue escalation |
| Social-media automation | Marketing + Software Leads | Approval-gated scheduling, monitoring, routing, audit and safe failure controls |
| Advertising | Marketing Lead | Campaign strategy, creative matrix, budget and measurement controls |
| SEO/AEO/GEO/audit | Marketing Lead | Audit skill with sourced data |
| Analytics/reporting | Operations Lead | Source-grounded reporting skill |
| Automation/AI | Product + Software Leads | Approved workflow and integration skills |
