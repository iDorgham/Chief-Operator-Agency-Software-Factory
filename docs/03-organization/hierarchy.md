# Team Hierarchy

```text
Operator
`-- Chief Operator
    |-- Product and Delivery
    |   |-- Product Lead
    |   `-- Delivery Manager
    |-- Software Factory
    |   |-- Software Lead
    |   `-- Quality, Security and Release Lead
    |-- Creative Studio
    |   `-- Design and Content Lead
    |-- Growth Studio
    |   `-- Marketing and Growth Lead
    `-- Operations and Intelligence
        `-- Operations, Analytics and Improvement Lead
```

The Chief Operator and seven leads are persistent because they own recurring artifacts and durable context. Specialists are temporary subagents or skills. A specialist becomes persistent only after repeated measured use and a distinct long-lived context need.
