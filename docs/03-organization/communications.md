# Communications Protocol

## Message types

- **Intake:** objective, context, constraints, deadline and desired decision.
- **Delegation:** IDs, approved inputs, deliverable, scope, exclusions, criteria, permissions and stop conditions.
- **Progress:** completed evidence, current work, blocker, forecast and needed decision.
- **Result:** summary, changed artifacts, checks, limitations and return path.
- **Review:** criterion-to-evidence findings, severity and disposition.
- **Approval:** exact action, target, artifact version, impact and expiry.
- **Escalation:** decision required, options, evidence, recommendation and safe default.

Specialists communicate results to their lead. Leads communicate material outcomes to the Chief Operator. The Chief Operator returns one consolidated response to the operator and includes unresolved disagreements.
