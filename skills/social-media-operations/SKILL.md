# Social Media Operations

Confirm business objective, audience evidence, offer, brand, accounts/channels, permissions, budget boundary, sources and approval policy.

1. Define channel purpose, audience segment, positioning, content pillars, formats and success metrics.
2. Build an editorial/campaign plan with cadence, owners, dependencies, production capacity, experiments and review dates.
3. Define the management flow: intake, briefs, production, claims/brand review, approval, scheduling, community response, escalation and reporting.
4. Design automation only for a named account and bounded action. Specify triggers, data, deduplication, rate limits, human gate, failure state, pause/kill switch, audit and recovery.
5. Keep facts, hypotheses, estimates and observed performance separate. Never fabricate reach, engagement, audience or attribution.
6. Public posting, replies, consequential moderation, account changes and advertising spend require exact approval unless an explicit bounded policy already authorizes them.
7. Return strategy, plan, management policy, automation specification and measurement plan. Coordinate assets through the Design and Content Lead.

Read `docs/04-workflows/social-media-system.md` for factory ownership and operating boundaries.
