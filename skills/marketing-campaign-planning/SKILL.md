# Marketing Campaign Planning

Confirm objective, audience, offer, evidence, channels, budget boundary, timing and sources. Separate facts, hypotheses and estimates. Define message, funnel, channel roles, creative matrix, experiments, metrics and stop conditions. Return a plan; publishing and spend require exact approval.
