# Intake to Brief

Identify project/client, outcome, users/audience, evidence, constraints, deadline, success measures, unknowns and consequential actions. Ask only material questions and label assumptions. Separate requested scope, recommendation and non-goals. Produce `templates/project-brief.md`; keep status draft until operator approval.
