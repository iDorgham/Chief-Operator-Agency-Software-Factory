# High-Converting Ad Frameworks & Video Scripts

This guide provides proven advertising frameworks and bilingual script blueprints tailored for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and Meta Paid Ads.

---

## 1. Top High-Converting Ad Frameworks

### A. The PAS Framework (Problem - Agitate - Solution)
1. **Problem (0-3s):** Call out the specific annoying pain point.
2. **Agitate (4-12s):** Intensify why ignoring it costs money, peace of mind, or time.
3. **Solution (13-25s):** Introduce the product/service as the effortless cure.
4. **Call to Action (26-30s):** Clear, direct next step with urgency.

### B. The UGC (User-Generated Content) Testimonial Formula
- Feels like an authentic phone selfie video from a real customer or creator, NOT a polished TV commercial.
- Structure:
  - *Hook (0-3s):* "يا جماعة، لازم أشاركم الحاجة اللي غيرت روتيني الأسبوع ده..."
  - *Show the Product in Action (4-10s):* Close-up unboxing or daily use.
  - *Real Proof & Result (11-20s):* Showing the tangible before vs. after.
  - *Exclusive Discount/Link (21-30s):* "ولأول 50 واحد يطلب، في خصم 25% من اللينك اللي تحت."

### C. The Before - After - Bridge (BAB)
1. **Before:** The struggle the audience experiences today.
2. **After:** The dream scenario where their life or business runs smoothly.
3. **Bridge:** How your offer bridges the gap between today and tomorrow.

## 3. High-Converting Story Ad Sequence (The 3-Slide Story Funnel)

For Instagram Stories paid ads or organic story sequences:

1. **Slide 1 (The Quiz / Polarizing Question):**
   - Visual: High-contrast aesthetic photo or short 3-sec looping video.
   - Interactive Element: Instagram Poll Sticker (e.g. "بتفضل الشغل من البيت 💻 ولا من المكتب 🏢؟").
   - Result: Engages the viewer physically (tapping increases algorithmic relevance).
2. **Slide 2 (The Shocking Insight / Behind the Scenes):**
   - Visual: Screenshot, video demonstration, or proof metric.
   - Text: "المفاجأة إن 78% اختاروا البيت.. بس 80% منهم بيشتكوا من ألم الظهر بسبب الكراسي العادية!"
3. **Slide 3 (The Frictionless Offer):**
   - Visual: Hero product shot with dynamic swipe-up / link sticker.
   - CTA: "عشان كدة عملنا عرض خاص على الكرسي الطبي المريح.. اضغط هنا وشوف آراء عملائنا."
