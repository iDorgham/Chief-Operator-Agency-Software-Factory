## 1. The Core Creative Execution Framework

Whenever tasked with crafting an ad, post, story sequence, or strategy:

### Phase 1: Context & Audience Calibration
- Identify target tier:
  - **Youth & Street Culture (شبابي / تريندي):** Fast, witty, slang-smart.
  - **Corporate & Startup Masry (بيزنس مصري راقي):** Fluent blend of Arabic business and modern English.
  - **Family & General Consumer (عائلي دافئ):** Empathetic, trustworthy, "ابن بلد".
- Select campaign goal: Follower Acquisition, High Saves/Shares, Direct Messages (DMs), or Direct Conversions/Sales.

### Phase 2: The 3-Second Hook Architecture
- Never open with logos, generic greetings ("أهلاً بكم"), or slow introductions.
- Use one of the 4 proven Egyptian hook archetypes:
  1. *Pattern Interrupt (صدمة البداية)*
  2. *Insider Secret (السر المستخبي / اللقطة)*
  3. *Direct Pain Callout (لمس الوجع بذكاء)*
  4. *Dramatic Climax Opener (الفضول القصصي)*
- See [egyptian-copywriting-and-hooks.md](references/egyptian-copywriting-and-hooks.md).

### Phase 3: Multi-Layer Scripting (Audio + Visual + On-Screen Text)
Every video ad or Reel must provide three synchronized tracks:
- **Visual / Action:** What happens visually on screen (camera movements, subject actions, props).
- **Voiceover / Dialogue (VO):** Egyptian Arabic script with natural conversational rhythm + English translation.
- **On-Screen Text (OCR):** Bold, thumb-stopping 2-4 word captions designed for sound-off viewing.
- See [ad-frameworks-and-scripts.md](references/ad-frameworks-and-scripts.md).

### Phase 4: Format-Specific Packaging
- **Instagram Stories:** Use the 4-part daily pacing (Morning engagement sticker -> Midday value -> Afternoon proof -> Night DM trigger).
- **Instagram Carousels:** Use the 10-slide continuous retention layout (Slide 1 Hook -> Slides 2-8 Value loops -> Slide 9 Summary sheet -> Slide 10 Save/Follow CTA).
- See [carousel-and-story-templates.md](references/carousel-and-story-templates.md).

### Phase 5: The Follower & Retention Engine
- Structure multi-part connected series (Part 1 / Part 2 / Part 3) to demand follows.
- Implement comment automation triggers: *"اكتب كلمة [سر] في التعليقات وهبعتلك الدليل في الـ DM فوراً"*.
- See [content-strategy-and-growth.md](references/content-strategy-and-growth.md).

## 2. Egyptian Market Guardrails & Best Practices

1. **Avoid Stiff "Google Translate" Arabic:** Never translate English ad copy word-for-word into formal Modern Standard Arabic (فصحى معربة) for consumer ads. It sounds unnatural and gets mocked in Egypt.
2. **Embrace Egyptian Wit & Cultural Anchors:** Use light, smart Egyptian humor, relatable daily life situations (traffic, coffee culture, family dynamics, remote work challenges), and seasonal triggers (Ramadan, Eid, Sahel summer, White Friday).
3. **Offer High Perceived Value:** Egyptian consumers respond aggressively to clear guarantees, instant replacement warranties, free delivery perks, and exclusive package bundles.
4. **Make Saving and Sharing the Default Outcome:** Design content with dedicated summary slides, checklists, or templates that users feel compelled to bookmark for later.
