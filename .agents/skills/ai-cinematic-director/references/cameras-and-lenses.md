## 3. Focal Lengths & Perspective Distortion

| Focal Length | Field of View | Psychological & Visual Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **14mm - 18mm (Ultra-Wide)** | ~100° to 114° | Exaggerated perspective, distant subjects feel further away, objects close to lens look heroic or distorted. Excellent for sweeping architecture and dynamic action. |
| **24mm - 28mm (Wide Angle)** | ~75° to 84° | Environmental context, immersive feel, slight dynamic stretch at corners, grounded documentary feel. |
| **35mm (Cinematic Storyteller)** | ~63° | The classic street and narrative focal length. Balances subject with surroundings without unnatural distortion. |
| **50mm ("Nifty Fifty" / Normal)** | ~47° | Replicates natural human eye perspective. Neutral, honest, zero geometric distortion. |
| **85mm (The Portrait King)** | ~28° | Flattering facial compression, creamy background separation, ideal subject isolation. |
| **105mm - 135mm (Telephoto Portrait)** | ~18° to 23° | Heavy background compression, background appears closer and larger behind subject, shallow depth of field. |
| **200mm - 400mm (Super Telephoto)** | ~6° to 12° | Flattened perspective, subject isolated against a painterly wall of blurred color, sports/wildlife tension. |

## 4. Aperture, Depth of Field & Bokeh

- **f/1.2 - f/1.8 (Razor Shallow):** Only eyelashes or iris in focus. Dreamy background melted into buttery circles of confusion. Romantic, intimate, moody.
- **f/2.8 - f/4.0 (Cinematic Commercial):** Full subject in sharp focus while maintaining clear separation from background. The gold standard for commercial and movie shots.
- **f/5.6 - f/8.0 (Sweet Spot Sharpness):** High detail across subject and immediate environment. Best for group shots, street scenes, and contextual storytelling.
- **f/11 - f/16 (Deep Focus):** Forefront to horizon crisp and legible. Used for landscapes, complex architectural interiors, and epic multi-character scenes.
