# Lighting Setups & Environmental Mood Reference Guide

Lighting is the single most important factor determining quality, mood, depth, and realism in generative AI imagery and video.

---

## 1. Classical Cinematic Lighting Setups

### A. Three-Point Lighting System
- **Key Light (Primary light):** Placed at 45° angle to the subject, defines form and exposure.
- **Fill Light (Shadow control):** Placed opposite the key light, lower intensity (1:2 to 1:4 ratio) to soften shadows without removing depth.
- **Rim Light / Backlight / Hair Light:** Positioned behind subject to create edge highlight, separating hair and shoulders from dark backgrounds.
- *Prompt keywords:* `three-point lighting setup, soft octabox key light at 45 degrees, gentle fill light, crisp hair light edge separation`.

### B. Rembrandt Lighting
- Characterized by an illuminated triangle of light under the eye on the shadow side of the face.
- Evokes depth, drama, wisdom, introspection, and classical portrait prestige.
- *Prompt keywords:* `Rembrandt lighting, small illuminated triangle of light on cheek, moody dramatic shadows, chiaroscuro contrast`.

### C. Split Lighting (Side Lighting)
- Divides the face directly in half, one side lit and the other in complete shadow.
- Evokes duality, mystery, conflict, danger, or alter-ego themes.
- *Prompt keywords:* `split lighting, half face in shadow, 90-degree lateral hard light, high contrast noir aesthetic`.

### D. Butterfly / Paramount Lighting
- Key light placed high and directly in front of the face, casting a subtle butterfly-shaped shadow under the nose.
- Glamour, beauty, fashion, emphasizing cheekbones and jawline symmetry.
- *Prompt keywords:* `butterfly lighting, paramount beauty lighting, high frontal key light, sculpted cheekbones, catchlights in eyes`.

### E. Chiaroscuro & Film Noir
- Extreme contrast between deep pitch shadows (crushed blacks) and razor-sharp pools of light.
- Inspired by Caravaggio and 1940s detective thrillers.
- *Prompt keywords:* `dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, Venetian blinds shadow pattern across face, deep tenebrism, harsh light beams piercing darkness`.

## 2. Natural & Environmental Lighting

| Lighting State | Color Temp (Kelvin) | Visual Vibe & Prompt Modifiers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Golden Hour** | 3000K - 3500K | Low angle sun, long warm amber shadows, warm skin glow, rim-lit hair, nostalgic, warm emotional resonance. |
| **Blue Hour (Twilight)** | 8000K - 10000K | Deep navy blue sky, cool ambient light, melancholic, serene, modern urban contrast against warm interior window lights. |
| **Overcast / Diffused** | 6500K | Giant softbox effect, zero harsh shadows, ultra-soft even skin tone, realistic UK/European cinematic overcast, melancholic or neutral. |
| **Hard Midday Sun** | 5500K | Harsh vertical shadows, high contrast, specular sweat highlights, gritty desert or tropical realism. |
| **Moonlight / Nocturne** | 4500K - 7000K | Pale cool silver or cyan wash, deep shadows, rim lighting from lunar reflection, mystery, quiet night. |

## 3. Atmospheric Effects & Volumetric Light

- **Volumetric Lighting / God Rays (Tyndall Effect):** Visible shafts of light cutting through haze, fog, dust, or steam.
  - *Keywords:* `volumetric god rays streaming through dusty warehouse windows, atmospheric haze, Tyndall scattering, sunbeams`.
- **Atmospheric Smoke / Cigarette Smoke / Fog:** Creates depth layers and lowers contrast in distant layers.
  - *Keywords:* `wisps of curling smoke caught in a single beam of light, heavy cinematic mist, layered atmospheric depth`.
- **Rain & Wet Surfaces:** Enhances light reflections, asphalt glisten, neon bounce.
  - *Keywords:* `wet asphalt reflecting neon signage, puddles shimmering with colorful highlights, rain droplets clinging to glass`.

---

## 4. Modern Stylized & Color Lighting

- **Cyberpunk / Neo-Tokyo Neon:**
  - High saturation dual-tone lighting (Cyan & Magenta / Teal & Orange / Acid Green & Electric Purple).
  - *Keywords:* `bi-color neon lighting, electric magenta rim light, cool cyan key light, glowing street signs reflection`.
- **Motivated Practical Lighting:**
  - Light sources visible inside the scene doing the work (computer monitors, candles, streetlamps, open refrigerator, smartphone glow).
  - *Keywords:* `lit purely by glowing laptop screen in dark room, cold blue light reflecting on glasses, ambient darkness`.

---

## 5. Location & Set Design Formulas

When building scenes, specify:
1. **Architecture & Era:** E.g., brutalist concrete interior, minimalist Scandinavian penthouse, bustling medieval Cairo bazaar with wooden mashrabiya.
2. **Surface Textures:** E.g., weathered limestone, polished wet marble, peeling wallpaper, brushed titanium, distressed raw linen.
3. **Depth Cues:** Foreground framing (blurry leaves or doorway), midground subject, background architectural receding lines.

*Example prompt:*
```text
An elegant Egyptian tech executive seated at a minimalist walnut desk, modern Cairo high-rise office at dusk in background, floor-to-ceiling glass overlooking the Nile River, warm tungsten desk lamp acting as practical key light, cool blue twilight ambient fill through the glass, volumetric city light bokeh, shot on Sony Venice 2, 50mm f/1.8, cinematic color grade
```
